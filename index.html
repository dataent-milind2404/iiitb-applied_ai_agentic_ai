02

Learner journey

The nine stages a learner moves through, from first enquiry to the start of the programme.

  1. Lead capture and assignment

    Enquiries arrive from the website, referrals and marketing campaigns. Each one is assigned to an admissions counsellor.

  2. First counselling call

    Quick first contact, understanding the learner's background and goals, and recommending the right programme.

  3. Second counselling call

    Conversations with alumni, coaches or faculty where useful, and answers to remaining concerns.

  4. Application and block payment

    Application, test or interview where IIIT Bangalore requires one, shortlisting, offer letter and block payment.

  5. Programme access

    Automated emails confirm the payment and explain how to log in to the learning platform.

  6. Balance payment

    Paid directly by card, net banking or UPI, or through finance partners offering no-cost and standard EMI.

    Turnaround: [7 days]
  7. Document submission

    Documents as listed in IIIT Bangalore's guidelines: [list or link].

    Turnaround: [7 days]
  8. Onboarding call

    Welcome call that confirms programme details and answers first questions.

    Within [3 days] of block payment
  9. Post enrolment

    Cohort assignment, the welcome webinar, and access to recorded and live content.

Onboarding call checklist

Welcome and basics

  • Confirm the learner's identity
  • Introduce the onboarding team; the call takes 10 to 15 minutes
  • Ask what the learner wants: upskilling or a career move
  • Confirm programme name, duration, batch start date, delivery mode and language
  • Documents due within [7 days] of block payment, or the learner may be deferred

07

Live session guidelines

Suggested do's and don'ts for faculty taking live sessions.

Full SOP document: [link]

Do

  • Join from the Zoom desktop app, which is more stable and has all host features
  • Check audio, video and screen sharing 5 to 10 minutes before the start
  • Use a wired connection or reliable high-speed Wi-Fi
  • Keep background noise low
  • Let the moderator manage chat and incoming questions

Don't

  • End the meeting yourself; the upGrad team closes the session
  • Switch networks or devices mid-session unless you have to
  • Run past the scheduled end without telling the moderator
